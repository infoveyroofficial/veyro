Your privacy.

Project inquiries

When you submit a project inquiry, your name, email address, business name, selected plan, Care preference and message are sent to FormSubmit and forwarded to info.veyro.official@gmail.com. Veyro uses this information to respond to your project inquiry. Downloading a brief only saves a file on your device and does not submit it. Please do not send sensitive information.

Optional external services

FormSubmit processes submissions and may use a spam-verification step. Its privacy policy applies to that processing. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process ordinary access logs. If WhatsApp is configured, following that link opens WhatsApp and its privacy policy applies.

Concept experiences

Demo businesses, prices, architecture and hospitality content are fictional. The retreat form only generates an on-screen preview and does not make a booking.

Contact

For questions about an inquiry or to request deletion of information held by Veyro, email info.veyro.official@gmail.com. Third-party providers may retain records under their own policies.

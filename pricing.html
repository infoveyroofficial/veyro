A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. You can pay the one-time website fee and manage it yourself. Optional Care adds agreed hosting/management support, minor edits and the reports listed above.

Who owns my domain?

You do. Register it in your own account. Domain registration and renewal are separate from our website and Care fees.

What does a revision include?

A consolidated round of feedback within the agreed design and scope. New features or a change of direction are quoted separately.

What about booking, forms and analytics?

We select supported third-party services during scoping. Paid subscriptions are separate, and account eligibility and consent requirements must be met. This showcase does not collect analytics or process bookings.

What are the payment milestones?

Milestones, delivery dates, hosting details and the final scope are agreed in a written proposal before work begins.
